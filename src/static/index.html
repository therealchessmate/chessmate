Column meaning:
• Term – individual evaluation component.
• White MG / EG – mid-game and end-game score for White.
• Black MG / EG – mid-game and end-game score for Black.
• Total MG / EG – combined differential (White − Black).
Positive values favour White; negative favour Black.

Top 5 Moves:

    Legend of Evaluation Terms

    • Material – raw piece balance.
    • Imbalance – structural difference in piece composition (e.g. 2 knights vs bishop + pawn).
    • Pawns – Queens – piece-specific activity and coordination for each type.
    • Mobility – number and quality of legal moves available.
    • King safety – exposure of the king to attack and shielded squares.
    • Threats – direct and potential attacks on opponent’s pieces.
    • Passed / Space / Winnable – positional and end-game control factors.
